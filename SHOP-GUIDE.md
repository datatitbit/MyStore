# MyStore — Guide for the Shop (one page)

Keep this page near the phone.

## Log in
1. Open the **MyStore** app.
2. If it opens straight to the **PIN pad**, that is your name already selected (fast login) — just type your **4-digit PIN** (default is **1234**). To change user, tap **← Back** and select a name from the dropdown, then **Continue →**.
3. You see your name at the top — you are logged in.

## Record a sale in seconds (Sales tab)
1. Tap **Sales** at the bottom.
2. On the **Quick Sale** card, tap **＋** next to each product sold (tap **−** to correct). The **Total** adds up by itself.
3. Tap **Save All** — every line is saved at once and stock goes down **automatically**. A whole day's common items take a few seconds.
4. For something not on the list, tap **＋ New Sale (custom item)** — pick the product, enter the quantity, tap **Save Sale**.

## Record money spent (Account tab)
1. Tap **Account** at the bottom.
2. Tap **"+ New Expense"**.
3. Enter the amount, tap a category (like *Rent* or *Transport*), and an optional short note.
4. Tap **Save Expense**.
5. The **Account Balance** card at the top always shows this month's money in, money out, and balance.

## Check stock (Stock tab)
- Tap **Stock** at the bottom.
- Tap any item to **add stock** (Stock In), mark **stock used** (Stock Used), or change its **reorder level** — the number where the app warns you it is running low.
- Items turning orange with **"Low!"** are at or below their reorder level — buy more soon. The Dashboard also counts them for you.

## Mark attendance (Staff tab)
- Tap **🙋 Staff** at the bottom. On work days **everyone is already marked P (Present)** — you do nothing for people who came.
- **Tap a person's name ONLY if they are absent** — it turns red and shows **A ✗**. Tap again if it was a mistake.
- On days the shop does not open (see Settings → Work Days) the screen shows 🌴 and attendance is not counted.

## Dashboard
- The first screen shows **Total Sales (Money In)**, **Total Expenses (Money Out)** and **Balance** for Today / This Week / This Month / This Year.
- The small boxes count: sales made, expenses paid, stock items, low-stock items, and **staff present / absent today**.
- Use the second row of buttons — **All / Sales / Account / Stock / Attendance** — to show only the numbers you care about.
- The **Summary** card puts the key records in plain words for the time you picked. Tap **📤 Share this summary** to send it (e.g. to the boss on WhatsApp).
- The **Top & Slowest Sellers** card shows which items move fastest, which move slowest, and which items did not sell at all in the picked period — so you know what to restock, reprice or stop buying. Use the small **📦 By quantity / 💵 By money** buttons to rank them either way.
- Tap the big **📋 Summary — sales, expenses & stock** button (also under **More**) for the full one-page summary of everything in a clear table — money, **Top 3 best-selling items**, **Top 3 worst-selling items** (ranked **by quantity or by money** — pick with the small buttons), stock and staff — with a **Share** button.

## If the phone or app has a problem (paper backup — owner)
- The Owner taps **More → Paper Templates** and downloads the sheets: **Daily Sales**, **Account (Cash Book)**, **Stock**, and **Staff Attendance** (that one already has your staff names printed).
- Write records on paper, and type them into the app later when things work again.

## Learn the app
- Tap the **"?" button** at the top of the screen (or **More → How the app works**) for a step-by-step tutorial inside the app.

## Log out
- Tap **Log out** at the top when you finish. Always log out so the next person logs in with their own name — every sale is recorded with **who** entered it.

## For the Owner only

The first time the app opens, it asks for the **store / business name — this is required**. The owner name is already filled in as **Milly** (change it or leave it), and you create a 4-digit PIN. The app also creates **Sales Person 1** (PIN 1234) automatically — rename or remove them in Settings if you like. You can rename the store anytime under Settings → Store Details.

### Add an employee
1. Log in as Owner → tap **More** → **Settings & Users**.
2. Under **Add Employee**: type their name. The PIN is **1234 by default** — leave the PIN box empty, or type a different 4-digit PIN now.
3. Tap **Add Employee**.

### Change a user's name, access, or PIN
1. **More** → **Settings & Users**.
2. Next to each person you can: **Rename** (their past records follow the new name), **Make Owner / Make Sales** (change access level), **Dashboard On/Off** (hide the Dashboard and Summary from that employee — they land straight on Sales), **Reset PIN**, **Deactivate/Reactivate**, or **Remove** (their old sales stay in history).

### Work days and hours
1. **Settings → Work Days & Hours**: tick the days the shop opens (Mon–Sun) and set the regular work hours. Attendance is only counted on ticked days, and the printed attendance sheet shows them too.

### Store details
1. **More** → **Settings & Users** → **Store Details**.
2. Set the store name (**required**), contact person, phone, email and website. These appear on reports and PDFs.

### Products and units
1. **Settings → Products**: add what the shop sells — name, **price per unit**, and **unit of measurement** (kg, sachet, tin, box…). Edit or remove anytime; renaming a product also renames its stock item.
2. **Settings → Units of Measurement**: add your own units, remove ones you don't use, or tap **Restore Default Units** to bring back the standard list.

### Budgets, reports, backup
- **More → Budget Setup:** set monthly spending limits per category. The bars turn amber near the limit and red over it.
- **More → Reports & PDF:** pick a period (Today/Week/Month/Year) and a report type (**All / Sales / Account / Stock / Attendance**), see it on screen, then **Download PDF** or **Download Spreadsheet (CSV)** — the spreadsheet opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Backup:** Settings → **Download Backup** — do this every week and keep the file safe.
- **Google Drive:** Settings → **Google Drive Backup** — connect the owner's Google account once, then tap **Save to Google Drive / Load from Google Drive** (e.g. when changing phones). Setup: docs/GDRIVE-SETUP.md.
- **Cloud sync:** Settings → **Cloud Sync** — connect a free Firebase account so several devices share the same records automatically (works offline, syncs when the network returns). Setup: docs/FIREBASE-SETUP.md.
- **Restore defaults:** Settings has **Restore Default Units** and **Restore Default Categories** buttons for every key list.
